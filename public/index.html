Jodi the singles’ corner for your wedding

Free for every wedding

Five hundred guests. Somebody at this wedding is single too.

You catch someone’s eye near the dance floor, twice, and then the night ends and you never learn their name. Jodi gives one wedding its own private corner where the single guests can find each other.

Set it up for a wedding

How it works

  1. The couple makes a room.It takes a minute. You get a QR card to print and leave near the entrance or the food counters.
  2. Single guests scan the card.No app to install. They add a first name, what they are wearing today, and a photo if they like.
  3. They see the other singles at the same wedding.Only people who joined can look around. Aunties and uncles who did not join see nothing.
  4. Two people like each other. That is a jodi.Only then do they see each other’s Instagram or WhatsApp. A like that is not returned stays secret.

What we promise

  • Free, with no adsFor the couple and for every guest. The code is open for anyone to read, copy and run.
  • Contact stays hidden until it is mutualNobody can message you or find your number by joining.
  • Everyone here knows the coupleThese are not strangers from the internet. They came through the bride, the groom or their families.
  • Deleted three days after the weddingProfiles, photos and likes are all erased. You can also delete yours at any time.
